# 5 Abstrakt Marketing Group Alternatives for 2026

Abstrakt Marketing Group earned its position: 500 plus employees, more than 1,700 clients, a long Inc. 5000 streak, and a real model combining outbound SDR teams with marketing services, plus industry exclusivity by territory. If you are researching alternatives, it is usually for one of three reasons: you want a more senior team on your account, you want capabilities Abstrakt does not offer, or you want pricing you can see before a sales process. Here are the five alternatives, sorted by which reason is yours.

1. ISOVERTIC. For founders who want Abstrakt's scope from a senior boutique, plus the channels it lacks. We run outbound appointment setting and the full marketing system, and add custom development on a modern stack and TV and CTV media buying with in house creative. Pricing is published, the meeting commitment goes in writing, and the people on the call do the work. The honest tradeoff: we keep a short roster, so we are the opposite of a high volume vendor by design.
2. Belkins. For companies whose only gap is outbound. A respected pure play appointment setting firm with strong process and review presence. No marketing services, which is the point.
3. CIENCE. For data heavy outbound at scale. Large list building and research operation paired with SDR teams. Ask to meet your assigned pod before signing; consistency tracks the team you get.
4. Callbox. For global and volume programs. Long operating history, multiregion coverage, published case numbers. The most machine like of the pure plays.
5. Martal Group. For North American tech and B2B services wanting senior fractional sellers rather than junior SDR benches.

## How to run the evaluation

Whatever list you end with, force three artifacts before signature: the names and tenure of your actual team, the day 90 number in writing, and the ownership terms for lists, copy, accounts, and code. Abstrakt and every firm above can be a right answer; the artifacts tell you which one is right for you.

The ask: if the senior boutique with the wider channel set is what you came looking for, the pricing is on the site and the calendar is open. Book a pipeline call.
