# How Much Does Outsourced Sales and Marketing Cost in 2026

Nobody in this industry publishes prices, which forces every founder through discovery calls just to learn whether a vendor is even in budget. Here are the real numbers, including ours.

## The market ranges

Channel vendors: SEO retainers run $2K to $10K monthly at credible quality. Google Ads management runs $1.5K to $7.5K monthly plus your spend, usually 10 to 20 percent of spend at higher budgets. Standalone appointment setting and outsourced SDR programs run $5K to $15K monthly, with pay per meeting models at $300 to $1,500 per meeting depending on difficulty of the target. Web builds at agency quality run $15K to $75K as projects.

## The hidden line items

Three costs the proposals skip. Ad spend itself, which is always on top of fees, plan $3K to $50K monthly depending on market. Tooling, $500 to $2K monthly across data, sequencing, and analytics, sometimes passed through and sometimes buried. And the ramp: almost no program produces stable results before week six, so the honest budget is a 90 day commitment, not a month to try it.

## The in house comparison, priced honestly

A marketing manager at $110K to $140K, loaded to roughly $140K to $175K with tax and benefits. An SDR at $70K to $90K loaded. Tools, another $20K a year. That stack costs $230K plus annually, takes two quarters to ramp, and still has nobody senior owning strategy, which usually means an agency retainer gets added anyway. This is why the build versus buy math tilts toward outsourcing until somewhere past $10M in revenue, where in house leadership starts to pay for itself.

## Full pipeline pricing

Firms that run the whole system, site, content, ads, media, and outbound, price between the sum of the channel parts and the in house stack. Ours: programs from $8,500 monthly for the asset and demand engine, from $14,500 with the outbound meeting commitment included, from $22,000 with TV and media buying added, each on a 90 day ramp with deliverables in writing. We publish these because the question deserves an answer on a page, not a pitch.

## How to read any quote

Divide everything by meetings. A $6K outbound program producing six qualified meetings costs $1,000 per meeting; if your close rate is one in five and your deal is $25K, the math sings. The same program producing two meetings is triple the cost per outcome, whatever the retainer says. Price per meeting, then per closed deal, is the only honest lens.
