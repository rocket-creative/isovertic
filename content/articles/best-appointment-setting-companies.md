## Why outbound to scientists and clinicians fails at most of these firms

A setter who cannot read the buyer's paper writes an email the buyer deletes. A principal investigator decides whether to reply in the first sentence, and the first sentence has to name the model, the assay, or the phenotype correctly. A practice administrator decides based on whether the caller understands referral patterns and payer mix. Volume shops staff for volume, so the caller is reading a script written by someone who has never been in a lab or a clinic. Reply rates in the low single digits are normal in outbound. In technical markets with generic messaging they fall to a fraction of a percent, and the meetings that do land are with the wrong person.

## How to choose between them

Who exactly will work your account, names and tenure, and have they sold into your buyer before? And what number goes in writing for day 90? Any firm on this list can be good; the one that answers all three without flinching is the one to hire.
