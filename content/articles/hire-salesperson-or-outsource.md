# Should You Hire a Salesperson or Outsource Sales? The Real Math

Every founder hits this fork around $1M to $3M: revenue depends on the founder selling, the founder is out of hours, and the instinct says hire. The instinct deserves a spreadsheet first.

## The true cost of the hire

A capable SDR runs $70K to $90K loaded. An account executive who can actually close, $120K to $180K loaded with OTE. Add tools, data, and management time, real management time, because salespeople without management produce activity, not revenue. Then add the number nobody budgets: ramp. Three to six months to full productivity is normal, and B2B sales hiring carries miss rates that hover near a coin flip. Price the miss: six months of loaded salary plus the pipeline that did not get built equals $80K to $150K of cost for zero meetings, and then you start over.

## What the hire cannot fix

Here is the part the spreadsheet misses. A salesperson inherits your demand environment. If the website is weak, the content invisible, and nobody has heard of you, the best closer alive sits in an empty room. Founders routinely hire to fix what is actually a pipeline problem, then conclude sales is impossible in their market when the hire fails. The hire was never the bottleneck. The system was.

## What outsourcing actually buys

A pipeline firm at $8K to $15K monthly costs in the range of one loaded mid level hire, and delivers a team across disciplines no single human possesses: build, content, ads, media, outbound. The ramp is faster because the playbooks exist. The risk profile is different in kind: a bad quarter with a firm ends with a notice period, not a severance and a restart. And the meeting commitment, when you get one in writing, converts the gamble into a contract.

## The framework by stage

Under $1M: neither. The founder sells, and the money goes to the asset and inbound. $1M to $10M: outsource the pipeline, keep the close in house, because closing is where founder knowledge is irreplaceable and prospecting is where it is wasted. Past $10M: hire sales leadership and begin internalizing, ideally inheriting a documented system from your firm rather than a blank page. The wrong move at every stage is the same one: hiring a closer into a company with no pipeline for them to close.
