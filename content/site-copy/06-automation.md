## The problem

H2: Small teams spend their experts' hours on repetitive work

A 20 person company has the same duties as a 2,000 person company, with far fewer people. It still has to answer every inquiry and keep the catalog and the publications page up to date. It has to send the newsletter and report the numbers too.

The work goes to whoever can do it. Often that is a scientist or the person who runs the company. Those hours are taken from the work only they can do.

Most of that repetitive work follows a pattern, and software and AI are good at patterns. The hard part is knowing where the pattern ends and a person has to decide. We design that part.

## How we keep it right

H2: A person approves every AI answer that matters

AI does the repetitive work fast. It is sometimes wrong, and it sounds just as sure when it is wrong. In your field a wrong sentence about a model or an assay costs you a customer. For that reason every automation we build follows the same three rules.

- **A named step where a person has to approve.** We agree with you which steps a person must approve. Anything scientific gets one.
- **Sources you can open.** Every summary links to the paper, the record, or the row of data it came from. The person checking can confirm it in a minute.
- **A record of what ran.** You can see what ran, what it made, and who approved it.
