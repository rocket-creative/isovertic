## The problem

H2: Most owners have tried AI once and gone back to the old way

You opened ChatGPT, typed a question, and got an answer that sounded fine and did not help. Then a customer called, and you never went back to it.

The tool is not the hard part. The hard part is knowing which of your jobs it is good at and how to ask. You also need to know when to check its answer. A video does not teach that. An hour beside someone who uses it on real work does.

A shop with six people cannot send someone to a course for a week. So we come to you, and we teach on the work that is already on your desk.

## What you learn

H2: Skills you use the same day

- **Writing that sounds like you.** Replies to customers, quotes, follow ups, job posts, and posts for social media, in your own voice.
- **Paperwork in minutes.** Turn notes into an estimate or a rough list into a schedule. Turn a long document into a short summary.
- **Answers from your own files.** Ask questions of your price list, your handbook, or a contract, and get the page it came from.
- **Numbers you can read.** Put your sales or bookings into a spreadsheet and ask what changed and why.
- **How to ask well.** Give the tool the background, the goal, and an example. This one habit makes the biggest difference.
- **When not to trust it.** AI is sometimes wrong and sounds sure when it is. We teach your team what to check and how.
- **What never to paste in.** Customer card numbers, health details, passwords, and anything else you would not email to a stranger.

## How a session goes

H2: Listen first, then teach on your work

1. You tell us what your business does and which jobs take up your week.
2. We pick two or three of those jobs where AI helps and say plainly where it does not.
3. You do each job yourself, with us beside you, until it works.
4. You get a one page sheet with the steps, the wording that worked, and what to check.
5. We come back a few weeks later to answer what came up.

## Questions

H2: What owners ask about AI training

**Do I need to know anything about computers?**
No. If you can send an email, you can do everything we teach.

**Which AI tools do you teach?**
Mostly ChatGPT, because it is the one most people have heard of. We also show Claude and Gemini where they do a job better. The skills carry over from one tool to the next.

**Is it safe to put my business information into AI?**
Some of it, yes. Some of it, never. We spend part of every session on which is which, and we show you the settings that keep your chats private.

**Where do you teach?**
In person on Long Island and in New York City. We also travel to Connecticut. If you are farther away, ask us and we will teach by video.

**How long is a session?**
A one on one session runs one to two hours. A team session is a morning or an afternoon. A community workshop is about three hours.

**Will AI replace my staff?**
That is your decision, and it is not what we teach. We teach your staff to get the repeated work done faster so they have more time for customers.

**Can you build something for us after the training?**
Yes, if you want it. That is our automation work, and it is priced separately. Most owners do not need it. The training alone is enough for them.

**Can you teach a group for our chamber of commerce or library?**
Yes. Tell us how many people and what kind of businesses they run.
