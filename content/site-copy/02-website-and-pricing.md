## Why the range is so wide

H2: Five things that change what a biotech website costs

1. **Page count.** A 10 page site and a 3,000 page catalog are different projects.
2. **Who writes it.** Scientific copy written and checked by people who know the field costs more than copy you supply.
3. **Custom features.** Online ordering, customer portals, quote builders, and connections to your customer relationship management (CRM) system or inventory all add cost.
4. **Compliance.** Patient information needs HIPAA safeguards. Device and therapeutic claims may need medical, legal, and regulatory review.
5. **Agency structure.** A team of eight with an account manager costs more per hour than two people. This is the largest factor, and it is the one that has changed.

## What AI changed

H2: Why the same site costs less than it did two years ago

Design, drafting, and code used to be most of the bill. AI now does a first pass at all three in hours. What is left is deciding what the site should say, checking that it is true, and making sure it is safe to put on the internet.
