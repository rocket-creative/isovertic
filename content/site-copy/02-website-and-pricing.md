## Why the range is so wide

H2: Five things that change what a biotech website costs

1. **Page count.** A 10 page site and a 3,000 page catalog are different projects.
2. **Who writes it.** Scientific text costs more when people who know the field write and check it. It costs less when you supply the text.
3. **Custom features.** Online ordering, customer portals, and tools that build a price quote all add cost. So does connecting the site to your stock system or to the software where you keep customer records.
4. **Legal rules.** Patient information needs the protections in HIPAA, the US law on patient health information. Claims about devices and treatments may need medical, legal, and regulatory review.
5. **Agency size.** A team of eight with an account manager costs more per hour than two people. This is the biggest of the five, and it is the one that has changed.

## What AI changed

H2: Why the same website costs less than two years ago

Design, first drafts, and code used to be most of the bill. AI now does a first pass at all three in hours. People still have to decide what the site should say, check that it is true, and make it safe to put on the internet.
