## Signs it is time

H2: Five signs your website is out of date

1. The copyright year at the bottom of the page is not this year.
2. The newest news item is more than a year old.
3. A service or product you added is not on the site.
4. People who have left are still on the team page.
5. The contact page has a form but no name, phone number, or reply time.

Any one of these tells a careful buyer that nobody is looking after the site. Some of them tell an AI assistant the same thing.

## What carries over and what changes

H2: What a redesign keeps

- **Your page addresses.** Every page keeps its address, or we point the old address to the new page. Old links and Google rankings keep working.
- **Your best pages.** We first check Google Search Console, the Google tool that shows which pages bring you visitors. We protect the pages that already bring visitors in.
- **Your brand.** You keep the same logo, colors, and fonts, unless you want them changed.
- **Your domain name and accounts.** They stay in your name.

What changes is the writing, the layout, the speed, and the security. We also make sure Google and AI assistants can read every word.

## How it goes

H2: A redesign takes four steps and about a month

1. **Review.** We read your current site and your search data. We tell you what to keep, what to rewrite, and what to drop.
2. **Draft.** In about ten days you get the new site as a working link.
3. **Check.** Your team marks the changes it wants, and your expert approves the science. You get two rounds of changes.
4. **Switch.** We move your domain name to the new site. We point every old address to its new page, so old links and Google rankings keep working. We watch the search data for the first month.

Sometimes. If the site is built well underneath, a few hours at $125 an hour may be enough. We will look at the site and tell you which one it needs.

**How long will the site be down?**
It will not be down. We build the new site next to the old one and switch over in minutes.

**Our site was built by someone who has left. Can you get into it?**
Usually. If you own the domain name, we can replace the site even when nobody has the old passwords.
