## Signs it is time

H2: Five signs your website is out of date

1. The copyright year in the footer is not this year.
2. The latest news item is more than a year old.
3. A service or product you added is not on the site.
4. People who have left are still on the team page.
5. The contact page has a form and no name, phone number, or reply time.
