## Can't we just build it ourselves with AI?

H2: What goes wrong when you build a website with AI

Anyone can build a website with an AI tool over a weekend. The tools are good, and for a pizza place that may be all you need. Your buyers check every claim. For a company like yours, three problems keep showing up.

**Security.** In tests of more than 100 AI models, 45% of the code they wrote had a security flaw ([Veracode](https://www.veracode.com/press-release/ai-generated-code-poses-major-security-risks-in-nearly-half-of-all-development-tasks-veracode-research-reveals/)). One scan of 1,645 apps made with a popular AI builder found 170 that exposed personal data ([Cloud Security Alliance](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-ai-codegen-vulnerability-debt-2026)).

**Visibility.** Some websites show their words only after code called JavaScript runs in the browser. Most of the programs AI companies use to read websites do not run that code ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler)). A site built that way can look like a blank page to the AI assistants you want recommending you.

**Accuracy.** AI writes confident sentences about science it has not read. A scientist on your buyer's team will spot the one that is wrong.

If your nephew already built the site, we can still help. We charge $2,500 to review the security of a site someone else built.

## What we have learned moves the needle

H2: What gets a company recommended by ChatGPT

The research on this is new, and much of it comes from people with tools to sell. This is what the evidence shows today.

**Proven**
- Pages that arrive with all their words already in place. Most of the programs AI companies use to read websites do not run JavaScript, the code some sites need before their words appear ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler)).
- Being on Google's list of pages and ranking well there. Google says its AI answers use the same list and the same ranking systems as search ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)).
- Not blocking the AI programs by accident. Default hosting and security settings sometimes block them without anyone choosing to.
- Being mentioned on other sites. In a study of 75,000 brands, mentions on other websites went along with AI visibility more closely than anything else measured ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)).

**Helpful, not proven**
- Pages kept up to date, pages that name their sources, and the same facts about your company everywhere it appears.

**Not worth your money**
- An llms.txt file. In one study, 97% of these files were never requested ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/)).
- Anyone who promises to make you "number one in ChatGPT." There is no number one, because the list changes every time.

## How to get recommended by ChatGPT, in plain terms

H2: Four things decide whether ChatGPT recommends your company

1. **AI can read your website.** Your pages have to arrive with all their words in place. Nothing can block the programs AI companies use to read websites.
2. **Google ranks you.** Google's AI answers use the same list of pages and the same rankings as normal search. Good search work is still most of the job.
3. **Other sites mention you.** Directories, supplier listings, papers that name your products, trade press, and partner pages all count.
4. **What you say is specific and easy to check.** A page that states the model, the method, the data, and the source is easier to quote than a page that says you are a trusted partner.

Everything we do each month works on one of those four.
