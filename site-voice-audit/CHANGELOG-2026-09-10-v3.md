# Rewrite v3 Changelog (2026-09-10, evening pass)

## What changed vs. v2

The homepage was rewritten to name the category ("The Growth Agency for Healthcare, Biotech, Medical Devices, and Life Sciences") and lead with the four served industries. All 15 other pages were then rewritten to match that voice, category label, and industry framing so the through-line is visible from any landing page.

## The v3 rules every page follows

1. **Category label.** "Growth agency for healthcare, biotech, medical devices, and life sciences" appears in the opening of every page.
2. **Four industries named.** Every page names all four served industries at least once, so a visitor landing from any specialty sees themselves.
3. **Buyer titles in the opening.** CEO, president, VP of commercial, chief scientific officer, chief medical officer, medical director, VP of regulatory, practice administrator, director of billing, principal investigator, and their equivalents. No marketing acronyms.
4. **Named deliverables.** An article, a landing page, a Google Ads campaign, a meeting on your calendar with a written brief, a monthly report. No vague "strategy" or "optimization."
5. **Dual CTA.** Every page ends with two next steps: pricing page for card/bank-debit start, and "book a pipeline call, send us your best-converting landing page, in about ten minutes..." with the "hand you back your afternoon" close.
6. **Bylines.** Every page ends with the two full George + Kristen bylines, copied verbatim from home.md.
