## Why a Year

A 12-month term is not a way to make cancellation difficult. It is a way to avoid judging revenue before a buyer can reasonably close.

Search work takes time to become visible. Google gives a four-month-to-one-year window ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names)). In healthcare, biotech, medical devices, and life sciences tools and diagnostics, a new commercial relationship can involve scientific review by a chief scientific officer or principal investigator, clinical review by a chief medical officer or medical director, VP of regulatory sign-off, procurement, finance, and legal review before a contract is signed.

We do not ask you to wait a year without information. The monthly report shows what was published, what it did, and what happens next. The six- and 12-month reviews look at pipeline and revenue. Those are different clocks, and both belong in the agreement.

## What We Will Not Promise

We will not promise a flood of meetings from a small advertising budget. A qualified meeting a week may be the correct outcome at modest spend.

We will not say this is a match if your buyers are not already searching in specific language. In that case, the website cannot meet them at the moment they are looking. We will tell you before you pay.

We will not hide the price in a proposal designed to make comparison impossible. Choose a package, add what your team needs, and start when the responsibilities are clear.
