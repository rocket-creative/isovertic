# Core Message Foundation

Status: foundation doc, not site copy. Nothing here ships verbatim. Every line of site copy that touches positioning should trace back to one of the four ideas below. Written September 8, 2026.

## The one sentence

We build websites that get found by people already looking for what you sell, then we spend ads only on those people.

## The reframe the buyer has to make

Founders who raised capital learned marketing as creating belief. They pitched something that did not exist yet and made people believe in it. So when we describe capture, it sounds too passive to be worth paying for.

Second, in their heads the website is a brochure. It is where you send someone you already met.

We are telling them the website is the sales rep. It works the territory alone, finds the buyer mid search, and hands us a form fill.

That reframe is the whole sale. Once a buyer accepts that the site is the rep, the rest of the offer (catalog pages, long tail content, targeted ads) is obvious. Until they accept it, no amount of mechanism explains it.

## Why capture beats creation for our clients

Our clients sell to specialists. Scientists, clinicians, billing directors. Specialists search in exact vocabulary: a gene name, a CPT code, a regulation. Almost nobody competes for those terms. Demand already exists and is unclaimed.

The general marketing advice on social media is written for companies whose buyers search in vague language, where capture is saturated and demand has to be created. Different problem, different playbook. We are not in that fight.

## Proof comes before mechanism

Lead with numbers, then explain. Never the other way around.

ITL took two years to approve the per gene page idea. That was the cost of making the reframe without proof. We now have proof.

Working numbers (internal, from HubSpot and Search Console, September 2026):

- ITL form fills: 13 a month before, about 64 a month after (roughly 4.9x)
- ITL organic clicks: up 3.7x
- Sydra: a company that did not exist online before mid June 2026, now producing roughly one lead a week on about $1,000 a month in ads plus $2,500 a month in site work

Site rule still applies: case studies show multiples and percentages only, first 8 months, no raw counts, and a client's numbers appear beside its name only after written permission. The 13 to 64 figure is for sales conversations and this doc, not the public page, until ITL confirms.

## Two honest limits

Say these out loud on the site. They make the claim credible.

1. This works when buyers already search with specific terms. If nobody searches for your category yet, this method will not find them, and we will say so before you pay us.
2. A lead a week on a small budget is a trickle, not a flood. It is the right result for the spend. We do not promise a flood.

## Where this lives on the site

- Homepage hero and first section: the one sentence, then the sales rep reframe in two lines.
- /system: the capture vs creation argument, one short section, ahead of the six steps.
- /results: the numbers first, mechanism second, per the case study rule.
- /audit and decision guides: the two limits as qualification criteria (who this is for, who it is not for).
- Sales calls and proposals: open with the two ITL numbers before any explanation.

## Words we do not use for this

Creating demand, brand awareness, funnel, growth hacking, ROI framework. They describe the other playbook.
