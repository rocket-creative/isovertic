# Structure Templates

Copy the template that matches your format. Fill in the bracketed parts. Do not deviate from the opening structure; it is the Isovertic signal.

## Long-form field note (600+ words)

```markdown
# [Title — one clear claim, not a question]

**By [George Stoff, Founder and Lead Engineer | Kristen Coughlin, COO]**
**Category:** [Arguments about X | The stack | Field notes from Y]
**Published:** [Month D, YYYY] · **Reviewed:** [Month YYYY] · **[N] min read**

---

## What you'll take away

- [Concrete promise — a rule, a checklist item, a specific answer]
- [Concrete promise]
- [Concrete promise]
- [Concrete promise]
- [Concrete promise — the "where this doesn't cover you" honest caveat]

---

[Opening paragraph — warm acknowledgement of reader intelligence, name the anxiety or the honest big thing, promise the specific payoff.]

[Second paragraph — the honest big number with real citation. One concrete stat, sourced.]

## [First section — usually "What happened" or "What the rule actually says"]

...

## [Middle sections — the argument, evidence, mechanism]

...

## [Final section — the stack / the checklist / the limit]

...

## [The limit, and where this doesn't cover you]

[Two or three honest caveats. This section is what earns the trust.]

---

**About the author.** [One paragraph, third person, with a specific credential and one small dry aside.]

**[The CTA — see Step 6 of SKILL.md]**

---

*[Disclaimer if HIPAA / legal / financial — see references/disclaimers.md]*
```

## Landing page hero + subhead

```
[Hero — one specific claim, not a benefit. Name the reader or the situation.]

[Subhead — one sentence, one number, one payoff. No "we help X do Y."]

[CTA button: specific verb + specific artifact. "Send us your URL" > "Learn more."]
```

Example:
> **HIPAA-compliant paid demand for practices that got the pixel letter.**
> The seven-part stack we ship in ten business days, for $750/month on any tier. If your best landing page has a Meta pixel on it right now, we'll tell you in ten minutes whether it's a lawsuit waiting or already fine.
> [Send us your URL →]

## Sales email (cold or warm follow-up)

```
Subject: [Specific noun the reader recognizes — a page, a metric, a case name]

[Reader],

[One sentence naming the specific thing you saw / read / were sent about them.]

[One sentence with the honest observation — a number, a risk, a compliment about something real.]

[One sentence with the specific offer — 10-minute call, one artifact, one answer.]

[Signature — name, role, one link.]
```

House length: 4 sentences. Never more than 6.

## LinkedIn post (George or Kristen voice)

```
[Line 1 — one concrete observation from the week. No "excited to announce."]

[Blank line]

[3-6 sentences building the argument. Named number if you have one. Specific example.]

[Blank line]

[Closing line — a rule, a question the reader will remember, or the one-line takeaway.]

[Optional: one link to a field note if the post is a teaser.]
```

Never: "Here are 5 lessons I learned..." / "Thoughts?" / hook-and-cliffhanger structure.

## One-pager / capabilities sheet

```markdown
# [Client-facing title — what we do for THEM, not what we are]

**Who this is for:** [ICP in one sentence, plain language]
**What you get:** [The deliverable, named]
**Timeline:** [Weeks, not vague "quickly"]
**Investment:** [Real number or range, not "custom"]

## What's in the box

- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]

## How we measure it

[Leading indicators on a 30-day clock. Lagging indicators on a 6-12 month clock. See compass_artifact reference in isovertic-marketing skill.]

## Where this stops

[The honest limit. What's NOT included.]

## Next step

[Specific CTA — see Step 6.]
```

## Case study

```markdown
# [Client outcome in one line — not a testimonial, an outcome]

**Client:** [Named or "A [size] [category] practice in [region]"]
**Engagement:** [Length + scope in one line]
**Result:** [The concrete number with the timeframe]

## The situation

[2-3 paragraphs. What was actually broken. Real numbers where possible.]

## What we did

[3-5 short sections, one per system component. Named tools, named vendors, named stack elements.]

## What changed

[Specific numbers with a real measurement window. Never percentage-only — always the raw number too.]

## What's next

[The current phase of the engagement. Honest about what hasn't shipped yet.]
```

## Proposal section (scope + investment)

```
## Scope

We will ship the following in [N weeks]:

1. [Deliverable with the specific artifact]
2. [Deliverable with the specific artifact]
3. [Deliverable with the specific artifact]

## What we need from you

- [Named access — CMS admin, ad account admin, CRM admin]
- [Named person — one internal owner]
- [Named artifact — brand guide, past creative, target list]

## Investment

[Real number. Real terms. Real payment schedule. No "let's discuss."]

## Where this stops

[Named exclusions. What we will NOT do. What triggers a change order.]
```
